# Two-reviewer applicability audit

These are independent AI reviewers assigned mathematical and empirical review roles, not human professors. They exchanged critiques, checked a constructive proof and rejected proposed experiments when their assumptions or interpretation did not support the central claim.

## Bounded question and actual result

Before another experiment, determine whether the two existing ResNet pairs can contain the exact clean retention onset required by our independent-Bernoulli law. The minimum check reads saved train/calibration/test covariance and existing neighboring clean-grid losses. No new extraction, fitting, importance scan, noise point, resource search or symbolic fitting was performed. Stop after these two pairs.

In our energy-one scalar encoder and tied-ReLU decoder with free biases, nonnegative finite-second-moment features with positive means and nonzero covariance permit a first-order sharing improvement from either retention endpoint. This excludes a mono clean optimum in this class; it does not determine noisy risk ordering or all monosemantic architectures.

Training covariances are 0.0933814261 (hidden channels) and 0.1633140130 (class evidence). Archived angular neighbors corroborate the predicted direction. Applied to these finite training distributions, the proof excludes an exact retention optimum for all positive importance weights. It does not infer population covariance or certify a globally selected shared optimum.

Stop an importance-driven onset search on the unchanged pairs. A tiny weight rounded to zero is not a theoretical mono phase. This is a negative applicability finding, not successful real-model phase transfer or major novelty.

The reviewers rejected a full correlated-Bernoulli extension as an automatic rescue and a widened high-noise search as central validation. The latter's eventual calibrated crossing is already covered by the support/noise-exposure bound; even full-support orthogonal codes exhibit that effect. It cannot validate the critical storage mechanism. Earlier real outcomes remain unchanged.
