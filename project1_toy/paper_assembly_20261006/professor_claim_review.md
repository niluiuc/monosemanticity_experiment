## Preserve the project question

**For representations selected by clean training, where does monosemantic retention have lower corrupted reconstruction risk than feature sharing, and how does the boundary depend on feature frequency, importance, compression and noise?**

The requested object remains a phase diagram of `Delta=R_sharing-R_mono`, coupled to a separate diagram of which geometry clean training selects. Decoder policy is an indispensable specified coordinate of that comparison, not a replacement research question. The broad compression/noise/importance question organizes the research; the proved contribution is an exact two-feature/one-code-dimensional class and its declared parameter intervals. A broad question does not confer arbitrary-load theorems.

## Strongest defensible contribution statement

We characterize **clean-selected feature storage and the associated mono-versus-sharing reconstruction-risk boundary** in a controlled nonlinear bottleneck. A globally justified storage transition gives the geometry; composition with Gaussian corruption yields an analytic critical risk boundary. Symmetric decoder calibration changes its coefficient, and a singular endpoint demonstrates why specifying evaluation policy is necessary to interpret the phase diagram. A fixed learned-activation pilot supports a relative-risk policy effect but does not resolve a held-out risk reversal.

This framing retains the original phase-boundary ambition while using the policy result to sharpen its meaning. Leading exclusively with “calibration affects robustness” would undersell the training-selected phase construction and invite a triviality objection. Conversely, claiming a general mono/poly phase diagram across arbitrary compression would oversell the existing proof.

## Theorem hierarchy: dependencies and scope

1. **Model and comparison definition.** Independent Bernoulli features, common activation probability p, importances(1,eta), scalar code, encoder energy1, tied ReLU decoder with free biases, Gaussian noise injected in the code. Define the coordinate-retaining mono class explicitly, include its omitted-feature loss, and separately define frozen-clean-bias and symmetric noisy-bias-calibration policies. These are definitions/assumptions, not contributions.
2. **Global clean storage selection.** On the proved rectangle eta∈[12/25,13/25], p∈[7/20,21/50], certify every sign, importance assignment and scalar bias activation region. The clean storage threshold pc(eta) separates important-coordinate mono from the unique opposite-sign mixed branch. This removes the central ambiguity of choosing a favorable geometry by hand.
3. **Critical geometry and clean gain.** For epsilon=pc-p>0, the globally selected weak amplitude is K_eta epsilon+O(epsilon²), weak energy is order epsilon², and weighted clean advantage is C_eta epsilon³+O(epsilon⁴). This follows from the exact branch and nondegenerate stationarity equation; it is derived rather than fitted. Frame it as an explanatory intermediate result, not a separate disconnected theorem.
4. **Critical mono/share risk boundary.** For sigma=t epsilon^(3/2), normalized risk difference tends -C_eta+B_j(eta)t² under each declared policy j. Positive coefficients yield the corresponding crossing scale and bracketing sign prediction, uniformly on the declared importance interval. State “at least one asymptotic crossing” rather than unique global crossing count. The full finite maps already show why an all-noise monotonic picture would be false or unsupported.
5. **Endpoint policy contrast.** At eta2/3, pc1/2, global clean selection is separately proved on .495≤p<.5. Frozen coefficient vanishes on the preceding critical scale, while calibrated coefficient remains positive. Identical geometry/task/noise can therefore have opposite asymptotic orderings under the two policies. This is a sharp boundary/scope result, not the entire paper's replacement objective.

The generic Gaussian moments, scalar bias profiling and interval curvature machinery support these claims. They should be explained or cited appropriately, not promoted individually as new conceptual discoveries. The general arbitrary-load bound remains a scoped analytical tool; the unresolved eight-feature training arm does not validate arbitrary-load phase predictions. Recursive-training results do not belong in this Project1 claim hierarchy unless separately completed and justified.

Explain why frequency, importance and compression matter, why selected geometry and matched comparison are essential, and why decoder policy must be stated. End with the exact class, analytic phase-law contribution and learned-pilot limit rather than a sweeping claim about all neural representations.
2. **Setup and prior work boundary.** Specify task, concept basis, capacity/energy, decoder, corruption and two policies. Distinguish storage transition from risk-equality boundary. Compare only assumption-compatible prior work and list the exact result not yet established by that work after the separate novelty audit. Do not present the standard architecture or a collection of familiar formulas as novelty.
3. **Training selects the representation.** State the global clean theorem, provide proof idea covering sign/order/activation competitors, and explain the small shared feature's birth. Put full exhaustive algebra in the appendix. Main text needs enough coverage to prevent a stationary-branch/global-optimum confusion.
4. **The mono/share risk phase diagram.** State uniform critical laws, show clean gain versus corruption penalty, and display analytic/prescribed numerical boundaries with failed/ambiguous cases visible. Above the clean threshold the unrestricted trained optimum itself becomes mono; this equality region must not be mislabeled a noisy sign reversal. Distinguish a local critical boundary from a solved all-noise phase map.
5. **Evaluation policy changes the boundary.** Present coefficient change under symmetric calibration and the singular endpoint consequence. This strengthens the original phase interpretation: encoder geometry is held fixed while the evaluation protocol changes the comparison. It is not a trick using unequal decoder adaptation rights.
6. **One learned-feature transfer and its limits.** Fixed frozen ResNet/CIFAR activation protocol, independent audits, paired policy contrast and zero/positive contribution decomposition. Explicitly report failed held-out clean advantage, unresolved risk-ordering intervals and absence of ordering reversal. The learned same-sign geometry differs from the Bernoulli opposite-sign branch; no real critical exponent is claimed.
7. **Discussion and conclusion.** State what exact phase structure and policy dependence have been established and what semantic/full-model/arbitrary-load evidence is missing. Preserve the organizing general question as future scope, without proposing another round of uncontrolled toy cases.

## Comparison transparency that is indispensable

- **Matched resources are specific, not universal.** Both models have scalar code width and encoder squared energy1; tying the decoder also fixes its total squared norm. The same absolute Gaussian code noise is used. Output noise nevertheless scales as sigma|w_i|, so redistributing encoder energy redistributes output sensitivity. This is part of the model mechanism and must not be hidden behind a generic “equal capacity” slogan.
- **Omitted concept is a constant predictor.** Important-coordinate mono leaves the second tied decoder column zero; its free bias is the prior mean, with loss eta p(1-p) in the independent Bernoulli model. Every total-risk comparison includes that loss. Mono does not magically reconstruct both concepts without interference.
- **Comparator coverage is within a declared class.** Cover both mono coordinate orientations and choose using clean loss only, and cover both relative signs/importance assignments in the sharing class. Mono is not every possible semantically disentangled architecture. In the learned data, correlated channels could be predicted by an untied or richer downstream decoder, but the tied mono comparator cannot do that. Current results are valid for the chosen class and do not establish superiority over those untested alternatives.
- **Noise and risk are explicit.** This is weighted squared reconstruction under Gaussian code corruption. It is neither input-image robustness nor worst-case norm-bounded adversarial robustness nor general AI safety. Train-selected encoders are held fixed during evaluation; both models receive identical bias-calibration rights within each policy.
- **Do not confuse equality regions and crossings.** Above pc, the full clean-selected architecture becomes the mono comparator. Delta can be identically zero because models coincide. This differs from a nontrivial mixed-versus-mono risk equality below pc.

## Indispensable remaining empirical gap and stop decision

The current ResNet channels are operational coordinates, not validated semantic concepts. They support one relative-risk decoder-policy effect, but the individual held-out mono/share orderings remain unresolved. Consequently they do not yet support a paper-level claim that a predictive semantic monosemanticity phase boundary transfers to a real model.

The user-approved next empirical step should be **one independently established semantic-linked pair** from an existing real-model resource, chosen before looking at robustness outcomes, tested with a fixed protocol and the same transparency about geometry, decoder, noise and held-out risk. A concept association must come from prior labels/annotations or validated feature evidence, not labels invented after seeing the curves. It should answer whether the operational mechanism is relevant to actual concepts; it need not recreate the Bernoulli exponent. If the required resource or semantic validity is unavailable in the time budget, narrow the real-model claim instead of generating new SAEs or scanning pairs for a winner.

After that fixed test and exact-claim literature audit, assemble the actual phase-boundary paper and decide its claim strength from evidence. No additional optional toy family is needed. Novelty, generalization and main-track significance remain review questions, not consequences of the correctness of the existing derivations.
