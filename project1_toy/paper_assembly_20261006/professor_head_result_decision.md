## What this does and does not validate

The original question is mono-versus-sharing reconstruction risk after clean geometry selection. This test answers it for one named real-model evidence distribution and identifies a **sharing-favored region at the sampled settings**. That is real evidence relevant to the phase question, even though it is not the desired mono-favored reversal.

It does not validate the Bernoulli storage critical point, weak-column birth, cubic clean gain,3/2 boundary scaling, opposite-sign selected geometry, uniform importance-family law or singular endpoint. The scores are continuous, correlated and frequently coactive, and the selected geometry is same-sign. A fixed importance/noise slice far from any demonstrated storage transition cannot establish the theorem's near-transition mechanism.

Rectified class scores provide independently named supervised evidence, not ground-truth concepts, causal hidden features or monosemantic neurons. Their zero threshold is dependent on the checkpoint's additive logit gauge and means nonpositive evidence in that definition, not concept absence. The reconstruction bottleneck is an auxiliary compressed-score model, not an intervention proving full ResNet image-input or adversarial robustness.

The mono comparator omits one coordinate and reconstructs it with a constant. Its full loss is included and both orientations were considered. A richer untied decoder could exploit correlations between these class scores; the test has not compared that class. The observed advantage must not be sold as superiority over all disentangled architectures.

## Paper relevance and exact remaining gap

The strongest current paper spine remains the globally clean-selected storage transition and analytic local mono/share risk boundary, with calibration-dependent coefficients and the sharp endpoint as a clarification. The new result strengthens its empirical scope to independently class-associated operational evidence and demonstrates that sharing can remain advantageous under the same declared noisy reconstruction task. It should coexist with the earlier unfavorable/unresolved arbitrary-channel pilot, not replace its archive or be selected as the only reported real result.

The remaining empirical gap is precise: **no named real-model distribution has yet demonstrated the predicted training-transition-linked risk boundary or its critical scaling**. Validated hidden monosemantic features also remain outside the evidence. Calling this single sharing-favored slice a real semantic phase diagram would be an overstatement. The claim can instead be scoped to an exact controlled-model phase theorem plus independently labeled operational transfer observations; whether that contribution has sufficient novelty and significance is an exact literature/reviewer question.
