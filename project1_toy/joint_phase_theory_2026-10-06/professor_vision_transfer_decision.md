# Professor decision: bounded learned-vision transfer

6 October 2026. Review of `minimal_vision_transfer_protocol_draft.md` before extension. No dependencies installed, data downloaded, activations extracted, models trained, channel pairs scanned or risk comparisons executed by this review.

## Decision

Do not execute the draft unchanged or present its proposed result as the next strong result. Lowest-index average-pooled channels plus five arbitrary noise values are a blind, inexpensive probe, but their connection to the proved mechanism is insufficiently specified. A negative result would not resolve whether the mechanism transfers; a positive result would remain a narrow two-channel reconstruction example. This is not yet a substitute for genuine semantic monosemanticity evidence.

The smallest justified next step is a revised, prospective activation-reconstruction pilot in an existing vision environment, with a mechanism eligibility gate before noisy comparisons. Because the local environment has neither torch/torchvision nor a pretrained checkpoint/data, actual execution is blocked on obtaining genuine frozen activations. Do not spend research time constructing a new vision stack here unless the root explicitly chooses a bounded dependency route after confirming availability with the team.

## Specific paper-critical gap

The endpoint theorem depends on different decoder gate positions: mono has substantial target mass at its zero gate and gains from an order-sigma bias adjustment, whereas the clean sharing geometry moves those states away from the gate and initially benefits less. Global clean training selects sharing before corruption. Genuine learned-vision evidence must test these ingredients, not merely observe that two arbitrary coordinates correlate.

Average pooling can destroy the exact-zero mass of ReLU activations. Dense positive pooled channels can leave both models' gates far from the data, making the endpoint policy difference negligible for the entire fixed noise grid. The Bernoulli endpoint coefficients and exponent cannot be assumed for such channels. Importance1/2 instead of2/3 is permissible for a narrow mechanism test but does not specifically instantiate the endpoint theorem.

## Justified changes to fix before seeing outcomes

1. Use a frozen official ResNet18 checkpoint in inference mode, but extract the nonnegative **post-ReLU layer4 activations before average pooling**, at one predetermined central spatial cell per image. Take the two lowest-index nonconstant channels, normalize each by training RMS without centering, and freeze their identities. One observation per image avoids treating correlated spatial cells as independent images. This deliberately preserves the known ReLU-zero-gate ingredient; it does not label channels semantic concepts. Do not scan for favorable pairs.
2. Fix importance2/3 if the intended motivation is the singular endpoint policy result. It is a task weight, not an estimate of visual concept importance. The real continuous activation distribution need not have critical probability1/2, and no Bernoulli formula may be fitted to it.
3. Preserve the fixed train/calibration/test image split. First report training-only nonzero rates, zero fractions, coactivation frequency, RMS factors and code scale, and the actual clean sharing solution and mono comparator. The code must support continuous nonnegative targets rather than silently binarizing them. If the selected sharing geometry reduces to mono, or clean selection remains numerically unresolved, stop before noisy work and call the pilot non-diagnostic for the sharing mechanism. Do not replace the pair or search nearby weights.
4. Make noise units interpretable: fix the common noise reference from the training data as `sqrt((Var(x1)+Var(x2))/2)` after normalization; use the prescribed five multipliers `{0,.05,.1,.2,.4}` of that reference. This is distribution-based normalization, not tuning to a crossing. Both models receive identical absolute code noise. Save reference and absolute sigma values. The grid can still miss a crossing; its absence is not evidence of all-noise ordering.
5. Isolate decoder policy from finite-split fitting. For the primary comparison, clean-train encoders on train, then obtain each encoder's zero-noise optimal biases on the common calibration split. The frozen policy keeps these zero-noise calibration biases; the calibrated policy reoptimizes those same models' biases at each noise on that same split. Test evaluates both without fitting. This ensures the policies coincide at sigma0 and avoids mistaking ordinary train/calibration mismatch for a noise-calibration effect. Keep clean-training biases and their test risks as explicitly secondary records, not a replacement baseline chosen after results.
6. Report not only the two risk differences but the policy contrast `(Rshare_cal-Rmono_cal)-(Rshare_frozen-Rmono_frozen)`, per-image conditional losses and each model's calibration improvement. The clean advantage and shared gate-margin distributions provide mechanistic context. An opposite ordering is stronger evidence than a tiny policy contrast, but neither is a universal phase theorem.

These changes are justified by the theorem's gate mechanism and evaluation design before any image outcomes, not by a favorable empirical result.

## Optimization and calibration quality

- Clean empirical bias profiling must enumerate continuous-target activation intervals and their stationary points/kink closures; scalar binary probabilities cannot be reused. Use one fixed bounded angle grid and one predeclared doubled-resolution check, retaining all candidates. This provides numerical stability evidence, not global clean selection unless a actual bound on missed minima is supplied. Set a risk-resolution tolerance before execution; ambiguous clean improvements stop the pilot.
- Mono comparator orientation must be chosen from clean train loss only. Retain both orientations and all records; no orientation selection on the test split.
- Symmetric calibration must support arbitrary saved nonnegative targets. Gaussian moment formulas remain exact conditional on activations. Curvature bounds must include actual target amplitudes. For right-tail localization the upper endpoint needs `max(target)-min(offset)` rather than binary1-minus-min-offset; retain a valid far-left moment bound with actual target amplitudes. Independently verify a small predetermined scalar case before the full pilot. This is a necessary implementation correctness check, not an invitation to new toy research.
- Calibration fits the empirical calibration distribution, while test loss is a held-out estimate. Population global-optimum language is inappropriate. Save the gap bounds for the calibration objective, and compare them with the intended decision tolerance.
- Use fixed paired image bootstrap settings, for example2000 replicates with one fixed seed, for test risk differences and policy contrast. Report the full intervals and failures. Bootstrap does not certify a population sign, and model/calibration uncertainty is not included automatically.

## Budget and stopping decision

One existing checkpoint, one predetermined spatial cell, one channel pair, fixed256/256/512 split and five noise levels only. First ask for an existing team vision environment or exported feature matrix with source/checkpoint/data/index/transform hashes. Feature extraction should be one pass and downstream code one bounded implementation. Set explicit wall-clock caps before execution, including a calibration expansion/time limit; an exhausted limit is unresolved, not a reason to loosen tolerance until a desired sign appears.

Stop if genuine inputs are unavailable, exact-zero activation support is absent in both selected channels, clean sharing is not distinctly selected, optimization quality is inadequate, or calibration hits its prescribed limit. An absent zero mass does not disprove generic policy dependence; it means this pilot does not instantiate the claimed gate mechanism. Report raw facts and stop rather than scanning channels.

If the revised pilot is eligible and completes, stop after the five noise levels regardless of the result. A resolved policy contrast and opposite orderings would justify an identical independent reproduction, not a wider pair/backbone/grid search. A negative or unresolved pilot leaves learned-vision transfer open. The clean-critical family and endpoint theorem remain the proved results; this review does not promise that a two-channel visual demonstration meets an ICML main-track evidentiary standard.
