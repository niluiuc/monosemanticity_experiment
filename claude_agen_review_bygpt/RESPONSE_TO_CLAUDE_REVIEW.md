## Research decision

Close the current coefficient and controlled-image fixtures. More audit cycles, image-model repairs and searches for additional crossing pairs are not the next scientific step.

Retain Project 1: determine when sharing's clean advantage becomes a disadvantage under corruption, with frequency, importance, compression and decoder policy stated explicitly. The paper-critical mathematical target is a finite-noise boundary under a stated distribution family, with a controlled approximation or bound that remains useful when gates change. A formula using a complete empirical risk curve is not an independent boundary prediction.

Claude's suggested low-dimensional summaries and twenty-pair test are a proposal, not an established route to a contribution. Activity, covariance and a nearest-gate statistic do not automatically determine nonlinear noisy risk. We must first state the admissible distribution class and establish sufficiency/error control. A standard or previously known result can also have a compact formula; predictive compression alone does not establish novelty. Do not start a twenty-pair screen before this mathematical target is viable.

Once a derived predictor exists, freeze its inputs, validity domain and tolerances, then use a bounded independent validation. A factor-1.5 crossing tolerance is a practical benchmark for that test, not the definition of scientific novelty. Failure and inability to resolve a crossing remain reportable.
